## You Don't Mean Zero Gravity... Right?

I do (normally referred to as microgravity). This project was made possible through a partnership with <a href="https://www.seds.ca/can-rgx/" target="_blank">CAN-RGX</a> and the UBC Rocket Payloads team. Our motivation was to determine whether thrombolytic drugs (clot-breaking chemicals) function effectively in outer space. We were doing our part in the ongoing efforts to protect astronauts during deep space missions.
